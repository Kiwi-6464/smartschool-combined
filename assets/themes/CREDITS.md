Their source and licence are **TO VERIFY** before a store release; nothing was added from other sources. The fifty original Smartschool++ wallpapers (see NOTICE.md in the public repo for the list) are included as well; their licence is also **TO VERIFY**.

**Store release warning:** several owner-downloaded wallpapers (`blueaesthetic`, `gta6`, `lucia`, `macos`, `mbappe`, `surface`, `windows11`, `windows11blue`, `windows11abstract`, `applelogo`, `gameart1`, `gameart2`, `gameart3`, and probably `gargantua`) show logos, characters, a real person or operating-system artwork of other companies. They are in the extension because the owner asked for them. `gameart1`-`gameart3` were 1920x1080 originals scaled up. Do not put these in a public repository or a store build without permission from the rights holders (or remove them first).
