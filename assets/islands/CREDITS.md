# Island art credits

The six island images in this folder are renders made in Blender from the 3D
models below. Every model is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) and was found on Poly Pizza.
Changes: models combined on one island, rescaled, some colours changed
(school walls, island tops) and rendered to a 2D image.
